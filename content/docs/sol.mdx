title: 6. Solana
description: SOL
---

A version of Ethereum that leverages high powered computers as validators to offer more transactions at higher speeds.

It's designed to run 65,000 transactions/sec, but requires extremely expensive hardware as validators to support this transaction speed, limiting the number of validators.

Value was destroyed during the downfall of FTX, but recovered over time.

Grew significantly during memecoin era in 2024 thanks to pump.fun being hosted on Sol.

## History

Solana was launched publicly in 2020.
