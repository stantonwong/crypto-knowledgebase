Polkadot is another smart-contract based blockchain with a number of innovations.

Polkadot is one of the first to design a Layer 0, where it's designed to facilitate multiple chains from the offset. The multiple chains, called parachains, act like a Layer 1 smart contract blockchain like Ethereum or BNB Chain.

By being a Layer 0, it provides the following to its parachains:
- cross-chain communication
- shared security
- forkless upgrades

## History

Polkadot's began with a whitepaper from Gavin Wood in 2016.

Gavin Wood is an Ethereum co-founder who also coined the term "Web3". 

The project was officially launched in May 2020 after being developed by Parity Technologies and supported by the non-profit Web3 Foundation, which was established in 2017. 

Key milestones include:
- raising over $144.3 million in an ICO in 2017, 
- the 2021 launch of the first parachains, and 
- the 2023 release of the major OpenGov decentralized governance system
