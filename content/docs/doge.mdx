---
title: 9. Dogecoin
description: DOGE
---

More or less the same as bitcoin (in terms of functionality), but was originally created for fun.

Built a strong decentralized community who was interested in giving back.

Elon Musk got involved at some point, pushing the value up, but also removing the decentralized community that enjoyed using it to sponsor projects around the world to a project that cares a lot about it's price.
