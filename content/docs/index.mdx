## What is Crypto

Crypto is short for cryptocurrency, a name made to describe Bitcoin and similar digital currencies.

The reason the term "crypto" was included in front of "currency" was because the currency was secured by cryptography, which is security by using math.

## Why Crypto?

- Decentralized
  - not under the control of any government
- Trustless

#### Use Cases
This allows for decentralized exchanges via smar contracts, removing the people required to make trades. All trades are handled via smart contracts and algorithms, minimizing platform costs and maximizes speed and access.
