Chainlink is a project focusing on tying real world data with crypto.

Also known as an oracle.

Oracles are required to bring RWA (Real World Assets) on chain. RWAs are assets that do not originally exist on the blockchain. For example, real estate, stocks, commodities, etc. are all RWAs and do not exist natively onchain like cryptocurrencies. With oracles, which are essentially providing a real world feed of the prices of these stocks and commodities, you can bring them onto the blockchain for trading.

## Website
https://chain.link/

## History

Chainlink was launched in 2017 by Sergey Nazarov and Steve Ellis.

## Chainlink Competitors

There are not many Chainlink competitors right now because it's not easy to become a reliable oracle. You need to set up infrastructure for gathering data and multiple levels of redundancy.

- Pyth network
- Band protocol
- DIA
- API3
- Redstone
