XRP is a centralized blockchain by Ripple Labs with the goal of replacing cross border transactions.

XRP was invented before stablecoins were available. With stablecoins now being commonplace, XRP has lost most of it's utility.

## Scam?

XRP is technically not a scam, but with most of the tokens still within the hands of the team, they have the ability to rug pull if needed.

## My Thoughts
This is the largest cap token that I would never touch.
