The first smart contract blockchain like Ethereum to introduce proof of stake (Peercoin first introduced proof of stake, but didn't fully support smart contracts in the beginning).

Started by one of the co-founders of Ethereum, Charles Hoskinson, in 2014.

Cardano is known for taking a more academic approach, which means development is slower due to extensive peer review.

## History

Cardano's development begain in 2015 and launched on September of 2017.

After leaving Ethereum in 2014, Charles Hoskinson and Jeremy Wood set out their plans for establishing Cardano in 2015. Hoskinson had left Ethereum after a dispute with another co-founder, Vitalik Buterin; Hoskinson wanted to accept venture capital and create a company, while Buterin wanted to keep it as a nonprofit organization.
