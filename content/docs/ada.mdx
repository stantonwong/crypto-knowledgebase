The first smart contract blockchain like Ethereum to introduce proof of stake (Peercoin first introduced proof of stake, but didn't fully support smart contracts in the beginning).

Started by one of the co-founders of Ethereum, Charles Hoskinson, in 2014.

Cardano is known for taking a more academic approach, which means development is slower due to extensive peer review.
