A centralized version of Ethereum by Binance.

Technically, very similar to ethereum, with centralized validators running the blockchain requiring better hardware for better throughput.

## History

BNB launched on July 14, 2017 with an ICO (initial coin offering). BNB, the token, was initially created as an ERC-20 token on the Ethereum blockchain.

Mainnet Transition: In February 2019, BNB migrated to the new BNB Chain, becoming its native coin.
