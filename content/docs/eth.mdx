Before reading about Ethereum, I recommend reading up on Bitcoin first, as it will be referenced.

Ethereum is taking bitcoin and adding the ability to write logic into it, known as Smart Contracts.

This means that instead of just sending and receiving a token, you can also add rules. For example, it is possible to set a rule where if the price of ETH goes to a certain number, the ETH will get automatically transferred to another wallet.
