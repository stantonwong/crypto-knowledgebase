A centralized version of Ethereum similar to BNB Chain and Sol, supported by 27 validators and faster speeds.

Known for being led by an individual, Justin Sun, and having the largest supply of USDT.

## Website
https://tron.network/

## History

Tron was launched in 2017, initially as an Ethereum-based token, before forming its own independent blockchain by 2018.
