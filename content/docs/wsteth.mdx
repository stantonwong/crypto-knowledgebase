title: 12. Wrapped stETH
description: WSTETH
---

A version of stETH that is stable. Your stETH fluctuates because it gets updated daily with your rewards. 

wstETH will not fluctuate daily, which makes it more useful for DeFi.

To not fluctuate, wstETH nstead maintains a relative relationship with stETH where wstETH will be worth more and more stETH as time goes on.

## History

wstETH debuted on February 19, 2021 by Lido Protocol.
