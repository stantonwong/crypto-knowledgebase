title: 8. Lido Staked Ether
description: STETH
---

STETH stands for staked Ethereum, run by a protocol called Lido.

Lido allows you to earn staking rewards without having a full 32 ETH to implement your own validator. Instead, it consolidates ETH to make validators and then proportionally shares the rewards with everyone who contributed ETH.

## Website
https://lido.fi/

## History

Lido staking was launched in December 2020, shortly after the launch of Ethereum transition from PoW (proof-of-work) to PoS (proof-of-stake). The Lido protocol was built to offer a liquid staking solution for proof-of-stake blockchains.
